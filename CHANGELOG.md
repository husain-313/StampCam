# Changelog

## 4.2
- **All the text, from every photo.** "All Text from Photos" and Smart Suggestions read every page again, not just the first 6. Pages not read yet are read the first time you open Name PDF, and remembered after that. This also fixes PDFs made with 4.1, and older PDFs you added photos to.
- **Done is quicker.** The PDF is saved first; the text in your photos is read afterwards, in the background.
- **Each photo keeps its own time and place**, even when you shoot fast and a photo fails.
- **Clearer messages.** If a photo is left out and adding to a PDF also fails, you're told both.
- **Cleanup finishes for sure.** If the app closed while cleaning hidden text from old PDFs, it finishes next time. Text kept for PDFs you delete or rename in the Files app is removed too.

## 4.1 — Safer
- **No photo is ever lost.** If a photo can't be added to the PDF, it now stays in the camera and you get a message, instead of being deleted.
- **Private PDFs.** The text StampCam reads from your photos (for name suggestions) is kept only on your iPhone, never inside the PDF, so people you send a PDF to can't see it. Pages you remove take their text with them. PDFs made with 3.9 and 4.0 are cleaned up automatically.
- **Faster Done.** Fewer photos are read for name suggestions, and the Name PDF screen no longer reads the PDF again.
- **No freeze when adding to a big PDF**, and fewer repeated area-name requests on weak internet.

## 4.0
- **All Text from Photos:** under Smart Suggestions on the Name PDF screen, open "All Text from Photos" to see every line StampCam read from your photos, top to bottom. Tap any line to use it as the name, even when it isn't one of the top suggestions.

## 3.9.1
- **Fix: English text is read properly again.** Reading Arabic and English together made StampCam miss most English text (a page full of English gave only one Arabic word). Now each language is read on its own, so you get suggestions in both.

## 3.9
- **Better name suggestions.** StampCam now reads small text too, like a laptop screen or a far-away sign, and reads it from the full-size photos while the PDF is made. So suggestions show up even with Small quality.
- **Flash starts off.** It's off now; if you turn it on, your choice is remembered.
- **New starting settings** for a fresh install: time only, top left, white, Small quality, Save to Photos on.

## 3.8
- **Faster taps in Settings, Your PDFs and Review.** While one of these is open, the camera's level and location stop updating in the background, so buttons and menus react on the first tap again.
- **Fix: the right version number.** About showed 1.0, so Check for Updates always offered an update. Now it shows the real version and says when you're up to date.

## 3.7
- **Fix: Add from Library opens even when you tap fast.** Tapping + and then Add from Library quickly sometimes didn't open your photos. Now the library waits for the menu to close and always opens on the first tap.

## 3.6
- **Fix: Add from Library opens on the first tap.** In Your PDFs, the photo library now opens right away on top, instead of sometimes not opening until you tapped a few times.

## 3.5 — Simpler
- **Done saves quietly, always.** The "Name File After Done" and "Share After Done" switches are gone. Name or share a PDF anytime from Your PDFs.
- **Pages always fit the photo.** The A4 page size is gone.
- **Simpler stamp:** GPS coordinates and compass direction are gone. Date, time, area name and your note stay.
- **No more camera tips card.**

## 3.4
- **Photos added from your library get no stamp.** Only photos taken with StampCam show the time and place. Before, library photos were stamped too, and ones without a saved date (like WhatsApp images or screenshots) got the time you added them. This applies to new PDFs and to photos added to an existing PDF.

## 3.3
- **Arrange Pages:** open a PDF and tap the ⇅ button to change the order of its photos. Make any photo the first one (the cover in Your PDFs), move pages up or down, or remove a page. Tap Save when done.

## 3.2
- **Your PDFs is now a grid of covers,** like a photo album: each PDF shows its first photo, with the name and date under it, and the number of pages in the corner. Tap to open; press and hold for Add Photos, Rename, Share and Delete. Select still works for several at once.

## 3.1
- **PDF names in 12-hour time,** like **5.17 PM**. Another PDF from the same minute becomes *5.17 PM 2*.
- **Fix:** the lens buttons no longer show "2" twice when zooming close to 2×.

## 3.0 — Quiet saving
- **PDFs are named by the time of the first photo**, 24-hour, like **06.52**. Another PDF from the same minute becomes *06.52 2*. Rename anytime from Your PDFs (smart suggestions are still there).
- **Saved quietly:** with Name and Share after Done turned off, Done just saves the PDF. No "PDF Saved" message, only a short tap on the phone.

## 2.9
- **No more page numbers printed on the photos.** PDF apps already show the page number, and photos you add to a PDF later now blend in with the rest. The Page Numbers switch is gone from Settings.

## 2.8
- **Add photos to a PDF from your library in one go:** on an open PDF, tap Add Photos → **Add from Library**. Your photo library opens right away; pick the photos and they're added to the end of the PDF, with page numbers carrying on. No need to go back to the camera and tap Done.
- **Take Photos** is still there in the same menu, for shooting new photos to add.

## 2.7.1
- **Fix: page numbers when adding photos to an existing PDF.** New pages carry on from the old ones (like 4 / 4 after 3 pages) instead of starting again at 1 / 1. This broke in 2.6.

## 2.7 — About & Updates
- **About page** (Settings → About StampCam): version and build date, a privacy note, what StampCam is built on, and Developed by Husain Helal.
- **Check for Updates:** finds the newest version on GitHub, shows what's new, and downloads it. Then open it with your signing app (like Signulous) to install.
- **Copy App Info:** copies the version and your iPhone model in one tap, for reporting a problem.

## 2.6.1
- **Fix: deleting a PDF from inside it now works.** The trash button on an open PDF asks "Delete this PDF?" in a clear alert and deletes it, then goes back to your list. Swipe and long-press delete use the same alert.

## 2.6 — Quiet and clean
- **Silent PDF:** tap Done and the camera is ready again at once. The PDF is made quietly in the background, with no "Creating PDF" or "Adding location" messages. The Name PDF screen appears when it's ready. Your photos are only deleted after the PDF is saved; if something goes wrong they come back.
- **Name PDF is simpler:** no photos or yellow boxes, just a clean list of smart suggestions.
- **Better suggestions:**
  - Reads all your photos, not just the first three.
  - Text that appears in several photos ranks higher.
  - Skips junk like "Made in…", websites, emails, phone numbers and prices.
  - New: the area and date, like *Jidhafs – 14 Oct*.
  - No repeats (even when the same words differ in capitals or Arabic letter forms).
  - Up to 8 suggestions.

## 2.5
- **Camera Control (iPhone 16 and newer):** press to take a photo, and now slide on it for **zoom** or **brightness**, like the built-in Camera app. The on-screen zoom and brightness follow along.
- **Camera tips:** a short card the first time you open the app explains the gestures (swipe for lens, hold for zoom dial, tap and slide for brightness, double-tap to switch cameras). Show it again from Settings.
- **Glass sheets:** Settings, Review, Your PDFs and Name PDF open on frosted glass over the camera.


## 2.4 — Pro camera look
- **Glass controls:** the top and bottom controls sit on glass (Apple's Liquid Glass on iOS 26, frosted glass on older iOS).
- **Haptics:** a light tap on every camera button, a click when changing lens, and a click when the phone is level (grid on).
- **Gestures:**
  - Swipe left or right on the picture to change lens (.5 / 1 / 2 / 5).
  - Press and hold the lens buttons, then slide, for a smooth zoom dial.
  - Double-tap the picture to switch between the back and front cameras.
- **Shutter:** bigger, with a little press animation.


## 2.3
Fixes found while testing 2.2:
- **Name PDF:** page numbers (like *2 / 3*) are no longer offered as tappable text, and overlapping boxes (e.g. on round logos) are cleaned up.
- **Smart suggestions** skip text that was probably misread.
- **Settings:** the stamp preview is bigger and zoomed in, so you can read it.
- **Compass:** steadier — it keeps the last good direction while the camera points at the floor or sky, and skips unsure readings.


## 2.2
- **Tap text in your photos to name the PDF.** The Name PDF screen now shows your photos with every piece of text highlighted. Tap any of it (a shop sign, a number, a name) and it becomes the file name. Swipe to see more photos.
- Smart suggestions and your note are still listed below.


## 2.1
- **Date and Time are separate switches.** Show both, only the date, or only the time. (Your old "Date & Time" choice is kept.)
- **Removed the cover page.** PDFs start straight with your photos. Page numbers and photo notes stay.


## 2.0
A cleaner, more focused StampCam: one great camera mode instead of three.

- **One mode.** Document and ID Photo modes are gone; the mode bar is removed.
- **Tap to focus.** Tap the picture to focus there; then slide up or down to make it brighter or darker. A chip under the picture shows the brightness — tap it to go back to automatic.
- **Richer stamp.**
  - Note line (like a project or client name) on every photo, set in Settings.
  - GPS coordinates.
  - Compass direction the camera faced (like *SW 225°*).
- **Better PDF.**
  - Cover page with the title (your note), date, places and a preview of all photos.
  - Page numbers.
  - Add a note to any photo in Review; it's printed under the photo.
- **Smarter names.** Your note is the first name suggestion; the cover page is skipped when reading text.
- **New look.** Rounded camera preview, round top-bar buttons, a yellow Done button, a bouncier thumbnail, a summary card and a big Create PDF button in Review.
- Unfinished sets from older versions still open (scanned pages and ID photos load as normal photos).


## 1.7 (not released — included in 2.0)
- **Removed Document mode.** StampCam now focuses on two modes: **Photo** and **ID Photo**.
  - The document scanner, the page filters (Magic, No Shadow, Enhanced, Grayscale, B&W) and the "Stamp Documents" setting are gone.
  - Pages scanned with an older version that are still in an unfinished set open as normal photos, so nothing is lost.
