# 📷 StampCam

**Take photos. Tap Done. Get one PDF.**

StampCam is a simple iPhone camera that turns your photos into a single PDF, with the **date, time and location** printed on every photo. Perfect for site visits, inspections, deliveries, receipts, or anything you need to prove *when* and *where* a photo was taken.

---

## How it works

1. **Shoot** — take as many photos as you need.
2. **Tap Done** — all photos are combined into one PDF instantly.
3. **Name & share** — pick a name (StampCam suggests one from the text in your photos) and send it anywhere.

---

## Features

### 📸 Camera
- Flash: Off, Auto or On
- Zoom with two fingers or tap the lens buttons (.5×, 1×, 2×, 5×)
- Take photos with the volume buttons or the Camera Control button

### 🕒 Date, time & location on every photo
- Date and time in 24-hour format
- Area name (for example: *Riffa, Bahrain*)
- Clean bold text — no box, with a soft shadow so it's easy to read on any photo
- Turn each one on or off, and choose the corner, text size and color

### 🖼️ Review before saving
- See all your photos before making the PDF
- Delete any photo
- Retake a photo — the new one goes in the same spot
- Add more photos at any time

### ✨ Smart file names
- StampCam reads the text in your photos, like a shop sign, and suggests it as the file name
- Works with **Arabic and English**
- Everything happens on your iPhone — no internet needed, nothing is uploaded

### 📁 Your PDFs
- All PDFs are saved inside the app
- Open, rename, share or delete them anytime
- Also available in the **Files** app under *On My iPhone › StampCam*

### ⚙️ Settings
- Photo quality: High, Medium or Small (smaller = smaller PDF)
- PDF page size: same as the photo, or A4
- Ask for a name after Done, and open the share sheet automatically

---

## Install

1. Go to **Actions** → open the latest successful run → under **Artifacts**, download **StampCam-ipa**.
2. Unzip it to get **StampCam.ipa**.
3. Install it on your iPhone with a signing service or tool (for example Signulous, AltStore or Sideloadly).
4. Open StampCam and allow **Camera** and **Location** access.

**Requires:** iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

---

## Privacy

StampCam has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your photos, location and PDFs stay on your iPhone.
